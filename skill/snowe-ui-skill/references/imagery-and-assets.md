# Imagery and Visual Assets

Use when a design may need photography, illustration, product media, diagrams, generated imagery, textures or custom graphics. Start with the communication problem, not an asset tool.

## Decide Whether a Visual Exists

Write the visual job in one sentence:

```text
For [person in context], this visual must [explain / prove / orient / reveal / compare / make desirable] [specific thing] before or while they [decision or action].
```

If the sentence is vague, compare the page without the asset first; composition, type, real product UI, data or direct facts may do the job better. Consider only the relevant outcomes:

- **No image:** information, task speed, privacy, credibility, or type and composition already carry the experience.
- **Verified product media:** exact form, finish, scale, condition or operation affects a decision.
- **Photography:** people, place, use, craft, evidence or emotional specificity that can be shown truthfully.
- **Illustration:** an ownable world, abstraction, sensitive subjects, impossible scenes or controlled narrative.
- **Diagram or data graphic:** mechanism, sequence, relationship, comparison or consequence.
- **Product composition:** several real objects or interface states art-directed into one frame.
- **Generated imagery:** a truthful visual is needed, existing assets cannot meet the composition, and generation can be directed and evaluated.
- **Custom graphic or texture:** a repeatable grammar encodes product, brand, content or interaction more specifically than decoration.

Record why the winner beats the no-visual baseline and the strongest non-generated alternative. `No image` is a completed decision, not missing polish.

## Build the Image System

Define only the image roles the experience needs (product truth, proof of use, editorial story and place, orientation, explanatory mechanism, atmospheric identity, background material), and for each its subject, distance, viewpoint, light, color behavior, environment, human presence, realism, crop logic, caption or attribution, responsive treatment and repetition limit. A deliberate contrast between roles can be coherent; unexplained variation is not. Prefer real representative content, and never let one strong hero asset hide a weak catalog, article, empty, loading or mobile state.

## Art-Direct the Slot, Not an Isolated Image

Before sourcing or generating, inspect the composition and write an asset brief:

```text
page and decision role
slot dimensions and responsive variants
subject, action, and factual requirements
camera / projection / illustration viewpoint
subject placement, gaze or motion direction, and negative-space map
foreground, background, depth, light, material, and color relationship
type, controls, or product UI that must coexist with the image
crop-safe and protected regions
continuity rules with neighboring assets
prohibited errors, cliches, false claims, logos, text, and artifacts
performance, provenance, editability, and fallback requirements
```

Mark where the page needs calm space and where detail may live; a conventional centered scene rescued by an arbitrary crop afterwards is not art direction.

## Use Existing or Licensed Assets

Inspect repository-owned and brand-approved assets first. Verify source, license, usage rights, attribution, model or property releases and edit permission, and record source URLs and license evidence near the asset or in the decision ledger. Prefer official product media when exact appearance matters. Search-result availability grants no rights.

## Generate Only When It Wins

Generate only after the asset brief and a real layout slot exist:

1. Generate or edit for the exact composition, aspect, placement and art-direction system.
2. Inspect factual details, anatomy, product geometry, logos, text-like artifacts, perspective, reflections, shadows, hands, wheels, repeated objects and edges at full resolution.
3. Place it in the implemented layout at wide, pressure and narrow widths, testing overlays, crops, contrast, loading space and adjacent real assets.
4. Compare it with the no-image baseline and the strongest existing, photographed, illustrated or diagrammatic alternative.
5. Edit, regenerate, replace or reject it; effort already spent is not evidence.

Generated imagery never proves an exact product, medical result, property, person, historical event, certification or other claim it cannot verify. In commerce it must not misrepresent a purchasable item's geometry, components, color, scale, condition or performance; generated atmosphere may support verified product imagery but never replaces it. Keep the prompt and provenance when reproducibility, disclosure, governance or editing matters, and optimize only after selection.

## Design Custom Graphics as a Family

Custom graphics (diagrams, badges, maps, patterns, textures, illustrations, callouts, empty-state art, hybrid data visuals) start from a specification:

```text
semantic roles | primitives | geometry or perspective | line/fill language |
corner and terminal behavior | positive/negative-space rhythm | detail budget |
color roles | type relationship | scale range | motion potential |
responsive simplification | source/provenance | ownership and editability
```

Prove the family on unlike roles, not several versions of the easiest motif, and test simple, dense, small, large, light, dark and content-adjacent cases where they exist; the language should stay recognizable without turning every surface into one motif. Build precise scalable systems as vector or code; use raster generation for photographic, painterly or textured outcomes, never for a maintainable interface symbol.

## Layout Evaluation

Judge the complete page, not the asset preview: does the visual do its job faster or more convincingly than the alternatives; is the focal subject where composition and reading direction need it; does the crop stay truthful and strong at each pressure width; does text stay readable without crude overlays or dead zones; are product, people, scale and context credible; does it belong to the art direction beside real content; do repeated images add information, rhythm or story rather than inventory; are file cost, decoding, layout stability and loading proportional to value; and is useful alt text possible, or is the image correctly silent?

Mark the result `REJECT` when the page becomes less clear, less truthful, more generic, dependent on one crop, incoherent with real assets, or slower without equivalent value, and keep the simpler alternative.

## Delivery Evidence

Report the visual job, alternatives considered, source or generation method, selected crop and variants, provenance, in-layout viewports reviewed, accessibility behavior, performance treatment, rejected artifacts, and why an image or custom asset was omitted when that decision shaped the design.
