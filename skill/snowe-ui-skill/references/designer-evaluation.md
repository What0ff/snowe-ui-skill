# Designer Behavior Evaluation

Use to evaluate or compare designs: whether the work made strong design decisions, not whether it mentioned the expected rules. Automated checks protect contracts and invariants; rendered and comparative evidence evaluates the design.

## Evaluation Unit

Evaluate a complete trace where possible:

```text
brief and verified context
questions and assumptions
research evidence
decision graph
architecture and art-direction candidates
selected implementation contract
working artifact
wide / pressure / narrow renders and important states
findings, corrections, and rerenders
```

A plan alone can reveal anchoring or bureaucracy but cannot prove visual quality, interaction or responsive coherence. Keep the method separate from the [verdict](quality-gates.md#verdicts): a second pass without another evaluator is self-review, never blind or independent. A stronger report contract shows reliable bookkeeping; better generation or criticism still needs repeated observed tasks and fair comparisons.

## Evaluate With Findings, Not a Creativity Score

Judge findings against the rendered artifacts; neither a second agent nor a numeric score replaces rendered or behavioral evidence.

```text
KEEP | REVISE | REJECT | UNKNOWN
criterion | artifact/state/viewport | evidence | consequence | correction or acceptance reason
```

Do not average taste into a number. One severe failure in truth, primary-task clarity, accessibility, conversion or responsive behavior can invalidate an otherwise attractive result.

## Behavioral Questions

- **Problem and architecture:** Did the work model the whole outcome, business mechanism, actors, objects, content, decision moments, return and recovery, and conversion before naming a familiar pattern? Are candidates structurally different because of live trade-offs, or cosmetic variants? Could the architecture have been synthesized without a matching local recipe? Does navigation follow orientation and object relationships rather than an org chart or default header? Is the strongest rejected alternative real and fairly represented?
- **Art direction and coherence:** Is the thesis traceable to product, audience, content, brand, place or behavior rather than a trend label? Do composition, type, color and material, imagery, icons, interaction and motion reinforce it within clear role boundaries? Is there distinction without compulsory decoration, and does it survive real content and secondary states beyond one hero view?
- **Discretion and invention:** Were no image, no custom asset and no motion treated as valid alternatives? Did generation or custom work beat a simpler existing option in the actual layout? Did research introduce, challenge or eliminate real candidates rather than produce copied styling? Does novelty serve comprehension, identity, desirability or interaction rather than merely differ from the catalog?
- **UX and delivery:** Are outcomes, prices, claims, content, actions, states, focus, recovery and accessible names clear and truthful? Does responsive behavior transform sequence, controls, navigation, imagery, density and disclosure while keeping priority? Do localization, long content, missing data, zoom, reduced motion, input modes and performance limits survive? Did rendered findings cause real corrections, including reopening a weak premise?

## Cross-Scenario Comparison

Lay traces side by side and look for unjustified convergence: repeated topology, page sequence, hero construction, card rhythm, conversion flow, navigation, carrier, type voice, palette behavior, imagery posture or motion grammar; the same vocabulary with nouns swapped; identical candidate counts and rituals regardless of uncertainty; local dataset categories leaking into briefs they do not fit. Different briefs need not look different for its own sake, so accept convergence that causal evidence independently supports and demand that explanation; cosmetic variation does not count as architecture diversity.

## Custom Asset Evaluation

Structural SVG validation is necessary but not aesthetic proof. Judge custom icons and graphics with the [contextual icon claims](iconography-system.md#accept-the-context-not-the-drawing-sheet) at every target size and theme, beside real neighbors, in simple and dense roles and relevant states, against text, native or existing symbols and the strongest external candidate, and reject custom work that loses. Evaluating changes to Snowe itself (scenario portfolios, paired revisions, held-out briefs) is repository maintenance and lives outside the installed skill.

## Benchmark Acceptance

A showcase benchmark is accepted only when:

- the business and offer are concrete, truthful within the fictional scenario, and immediately understandable;
- architecture, content, conversion and visual identity form one product-specific thesis;
- representative products, prices, comparison or selection evidence and an actionable conversion path exist;
- imagery, custom assets and motion each have a stated job and in-layout comparison, and omissions are deliberate;
- wide, pressure and narrow screenshots plus important interaction and reduced-motion states were reviewed;
- every `REJECT` and material `REVISE` was corrected and rerendered;
- build, static, accessibility and relevant performance checks passed or their limits are explicit.

Store the brief, decision evidence, raw renders, findings and final artifacts so another evaluator can inspect the claim; a README screenshot is a showcase, not the sole proof.
