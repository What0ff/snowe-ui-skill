## Limits and transfer

The paired isolated host tasks are a separate bounded observation, recorded in
`HOST-PROBES.md` with source/receipt/snapshot bindings in `HOST-PROBES.json`.
In both independent rounds, the control substituted category proxies for two
external identities; the candidate used original artwork while retaining
custom product-symbol work. Both versions already produced coherent layouts
and useful labeled compact symbols, so these two small pairs do not establish
a generation/alignment improvement or reliable run variance. Three runs have
current reusable receipt bindings; one used native host captures and measured
browser metrics without a reusable receipt. These authored examples and unit results alone
cannot prove improved model generation, representative-user recognition or
generalization. Source role, owner/cause, predicted repair, current render and
applicability/counterexample remain the transferable reasoning contract.
