    03 / The promise

    A bike is not a box on a doorstep.

    We fit the first ride—and stay for the hundredth.

    01

    Personal fit

    Thirty measured minutes for frame, saddle, reach, and the clothes you actually ride in. Included with every bike.

    02

    Real test route

    Twenty traffic-calmed minutes with corners, a short climb, rough paving, and room to compare two models.

    03

    12-month care

    A 30-day settling check plus two seasonal tune-ups. Parts are extra; our workshop time is included.

    04

    Right-ride exchange

    Ride for 30 days. If the fit or use case is wrong, exchange within the Goodturn range. Clear terms, no shame.

    Every Goodturn bike leaves with

    • Lights, fenders, bell and lock mounts fitted
    • Shop pickup free · city delivery €35
    • 30-day exchange · 5-year frame warranty
    • A real mechanic’s name on the setup card
