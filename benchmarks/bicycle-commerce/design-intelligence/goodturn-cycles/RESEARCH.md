# Decision-led External Research

Accessed 2026-08-10. Observations are evidence for this fictional benchmark, not copied compositions or proof that any one live implementation is universally effective.

## Category and commerce evidence

- [Canyon city/touring model finder](https://www.canyon.com/en-us/bikefinder/city-touring/) asks whether the rider is commuting/discovering the city or touring/exploring nature before returning a model. **Changed decision:** intended use becomes an optional selection dimension, not a technical category gate.
- [Brompton test-ride journey](https://us.brompton.com/find-a-store/test-ride) describes a test ride as useful for expert tips, quick comparison, and final reassurance. **Changed decision:** test ride is a primary confidence conversion beside purchase, not a footer service.
- [Specialized current shop](https://www.specialized.com/us/en/shop/shop) exposes experience, size, material, wheel, and other product attributes and connects retail with service, demos, and fitting. **Changed decision:** Goodturn keeps use, fit, and service visible but avoids mass-catalog filter density because its range has only three bikes.
- [Baymard Product Page UX 2026](https://baymard.com/blog/current-state-ecommerce-product-page-ux) synthesizes current benchmark findings including exposed size selectors, in-scale imagery, total cost near the buy area, and accessible return policy. **Changed decision:** product details show size buttons, an in-use image, city delivery/pickup cost, reservation terms, and the 30-day exchange beside conversion.

## Transferable tensions

- **Guidance versus browseability:** help novices choose without hiding products behind a quiz.
- **Image desire versus product truth:** use art-directed editorial imagery for scale/identity, but keep full side-profile studio views for frame and equipment inspection.
- **Test versus transact:** let uncertainty resolve through a real ride while keeping a direct route for informed buyers.
- **Curated range versus missing choice:** make the three-bike limit a clear service promise and expose the intended use boundaries.
- **Workshop warmth versus technical trust:** pair tactile material and editorial voice with precise prices, specifications, availability, and ownership terms.

## Deliberately not copied

No competitor’s navigation labels, page composition, product naming, visual system, copy, imagery, or signature interaction was reused. Category evidence changed the sequence and proof obligations; Goodturn’s identity comes from its fictional workshop, product range, city-use model, and accepted art direction.
