Before you alter a home

Home alteration permit

Check whether your work needs permission, prepare the right evidence, and follow one request from submission to decision.

Form time
8–12 min
Decision
10 working days
Fee
No fee

Before you start

You will need the property address, a short description of the work, and a sketch or contractor plan. Owners and authorised tenants can apply.

  • Property addressIncluding unit and cadastral number when known
  • Work planPDF, JPG, or PNG; up to 15 MB per file
  • Owner consentOnly when the applicant is a tenant
