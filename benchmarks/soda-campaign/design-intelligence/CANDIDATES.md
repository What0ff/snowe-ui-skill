# Doppler Soda — Candidate Directions

Candidates use the same fictional offer, three flavors, six-pack conversion, accessibility contract, and static-hosting constraint. They differ in organizing logic and experiential thesis, not merely color or motion intensity.

## Experience architecture candidates

### A — Pressure sequence (selected)

**Organizing thesis:** establish the physical can and product category immediately, then move from `meet the signal` → `tune a flavor` → `understand the sensation` → `compose six`.

- Opening: proposition, category, price, all three flavor controls, and a direct pack action share one product stage.
- Navigation: Signals, The sensation, Mix a six; each label maps to a visitor decision rather than an internal brand department.
- Product state: one selected flavor persists through the opening stage and flavor section.
- Conversion: a complete 2/2/2 six-pack is editable in place; no forced configuration funnel.
- Responsive: wide is a product/type collision, pressure becomes a balanced two-column stage, narrow separates type and product vertically while keeping selector and CTA above the fold.
- Accepted trade-off: a linear campaign journey is more authored than a catalog, but every state and destination remains directly reachable.
- Failure assumption: the product stage may consume too much narrow-height space.

### B — Flavor instrument

**Organizing thesis:** make the page a tuning interface where visitors scrub a frequency dial to discover flavor, copy, color, and can state.

- Opening prioritizes an interactive equalizer and a horizontally scrubbed can.
- Product facts and conversion appear after a chosen signal locks in.
- Navigation is state-based rather than sectional.
- Responsive becomes a radial touch dial on narrow screens.
- Benefit: interaction and brand metaphor are inseparable.
- Accepted trade-off: product identity and commercial content arrive only after unfamiliar manipulation.
- Reject condition met: it makes a novel gesture the primary route to understanding flavor and pack options, contradicting immediate clarity and keyboard/touch parity.

### C — Campaign paper trail

**Organizing thesis:** treat the launch as a collection of poster drops, flavor notes, event fragments, and a corner-store order sheet.

- Opening is a typographic campaign poster with cans crossing between modular stories.
- Navigation is an index of drops rather than a guided product sequence.
- Conversion resembles a printed wholesale/order ticket.
- Responsive reorders modules into a dense vertical zine.
- Benefit: strongest editorial breadth and secondary content rhythm.
- Accepted trade-off: several equally loud modules reduce the physical can's centrality.
- Reject condition met: the site risks becoming a motion reel and poster archive before it becomes useful product communication.

## Art-direction candidates

### 1 — Compressed pressure field (selected)

Carbonation pressure and a rotating aluminum cylinder produce the visual system: oversized signal typography acts as a spatial boundary, horizontal wave bands compress around the can, flavor colors switch as fields rather than gradients, and metallic rims/highlights remain material. Unbounded carries stretched campaign authorship; Manrope carries controls and facts. The identity carrier appears only in campaign/product moments, while routine controls remain plain and high-contrast.

Risk: giant type and concentric graphics can overpower the brand/category sentence. Revisit if the first screenshot reads as an abstract motion demo rather than soda.

### 2 — Chromatic formulation desk

Flavor behaves like translucent measured layers, annotated ingredient relationships, and liquid cross-sections. It could explain blends elegantly and make switching systematic. It was rejected because laboratory cues can imply nutritional/scientific authority the fictional product does not claim, and transparent layers make the aluminum can less materially dominant.

### 3 — Flash-vending nocturne

Hard flash photography, black surroundings, reflective retail tiles, and receipt typography frame Doppler as a late-night object. It could create immediate desire and cultural context. It was rejected because generated photography would need exact can continuity across animation, flavors, hands, reflections, and crops; it also risks borrowing a familiar streetwear/energy-drink posture rather than deriving identity from Doppler's product behavior.

## Primary motion candidates

| Candidate | Product job | Strength | Decisive weakness / trigger |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Segmented CSS-3D cylinder + local SVG label + finite DOM fizz (selected) | Convincing can entrance, direct rotation, flavor-label state, pressure release | Dependency-free, inspectable, texture remains editable, static/reduced equivalent shares the same label system | Reopen if segment gaps, unreadable texture, interaction latency, or weak depth remain after rendered correction |
| Locally bundled WebGL scene and modeled can | Highest-quality camera, lighting, reflections, and physical interpolation | True cylindrical geometry and shader control | Adds renderer/model/tooling/license/maintenance cost; reduced/static and responsive compositions become separate authoring paths |
| Browser-captured video or image sequence | Photoreal repeatable launch choreography | Stable art-directed timing | Large repository cost, fixed crop, weak interruption, and flavor state cannot remain directly manipulable without parallel assets |
| SVG 2.5D can | Sharp, tiny, accessible static asset with robust responsive scaling | Easiest local/static delivery | Rotation and surface continuity read as a flat illustration, not the required physical product |

## Strongest rejected alternative

The strongest rejected direction combined the **Flavor instrument** architecture with a locally bundled **WebGL** can. It could have produced the most technically photoreal camera and lighting sequence, and its tuning metaphor fit the name. It lost because visitors had to operate an unfamiliar instrument before the product, assortment, and action became clear; the implementation also created a second, separately authored reduced/mobile product scene. The selected pressure sequence makes the same motion thesis legible at rest and lets ordinary flavor buttons and pack controls coexist with direct can manipulation.

## Selection summary

`Immediate product truth + one ownable physical carrier + reversible direct states + one static architecture` is the decisive chain. The winner is not the least ambitious candidate; it concentrates ambition where physical soda motion changes recognition and desire, while keeping navigation and conversion conventional.
