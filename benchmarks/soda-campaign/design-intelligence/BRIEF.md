# Doppler Soda — Benchmark Brief

## Status and scope

This is a fictional, repository-local Snowe benchmark. Doppler, its products, flavors, packaging, prices, availability, copy, and commercial terms are concept content. The work demonstrates one campaign-level expressive-motion capability; it does not alter or extend Snowe's stable architecture, schema 3 retrieval behavior, references, or methodology.

## Product truth

- **Offer:** a 330 ml carbonated soft drink sold as single-flavor or mixed six-packs.
- **Positioning:** a conspicuous sensory interruption for the hour between finishing the day and deciding what comes next. It is soda, not a wellness, energy, alcoholic, or performance product.
- **Audience:** design-aware adults choosing an expressive non-alcoholic drink for an afternoon break, a shared table, or the start of an evening.
- **Assortment:** three fictional blends: Sun Shift (tangerine and juniper), Pink Noise (grapefruit and hibiscus), and Night Signal (black cherry and toasted spice).
- **Conversion:** tune a flavor, understand the three blends, compose a six-pack, then receive clear fictional-demo confirmation. No payment or production backend exists.
- **Success condition:** within the opening viewport a visitor can identify the brand as carbonated soft drink, recognize the can, understand that three flavors exist, change the product state, and find the six-pack action.

## Experience boundary

The benchmark is one static campaign page. It owns product introduction, flavor comparison, pack composition, and local confirmation. Fulfilment, payment, account, nutrition, and real inventory are deliberately outside the prototype. The confirmation explicitly states that no order or payment was sent.

## Actors, objects, and decisions

| Element | Required truth |
| --- | --- |
| Visitor | First-time or returning; pointer, touch, keyboard, or reduced-motion context |
| Product | Doppler carbonated soft drink in a 330 ml aluminum can |
| Flavor | Name, blend, color/material state, can label, short sensory description |
| Pack | Six cans; adjustable flavor quantities; total must remain six |
| Action | Choose flavor, inspect all variants, tune pack, confirm fictional demo |
| Recovery | Flavor and pack controls remain direct, reversible, and non-gesture-dependent |

Decision moments:

1. `What is this?` → brand, product category, can, proposition, and pack price appear without interaction.
2. `Which one fits the moment?` → all three names and blends stay visible; selecting one changes product and copy state.
3. `Is there substance beyond the hero?` → product construction, occasion, assortment, and pack composition use real-shaped fictional content.
4. `What do I do?` → the mixed-six builder starts complete, permits direct adjustment, validates six cans, and confirms locally.

## Constraints and proof

- Static repository hosting; no third-party runtime requests, analytics, backend, package manager, or build step.
- Flagship physical-product motion, one user/state-controlled transformation, restrained functional feedback, and a complete reduced/static mode.
- Deliberate 1440 × 1000, 900 × 1000, and 390 × 844 compositions.
- Semantic controls, visible focus, accessible names and states, keyboard operation, mobile navigation, no horizontal page overflow, and no action that depends only on drag, hover, color, image, or animation.
- Local assets with license/provenance records and a final 5–10 second browser-captured README preview.
- Existing tests, deterministic contract regression, compile checks, browser smoke, and benchmarks remain green.

## Explicit non-goals

- No health, nutrition, sustainability, hydration, energy, or performance claim.
- No imitation of an existing beverage campaign or packaging system.
- No soda-specific rule, style catalog, motion recipe, dependency, or specialist reference added to Snowe.
- No claim that one rendered campaign establishes universal aesthetic quality.
