# Cross-benchmark causal comparison

This review compares Goodturn with three forward-tests. Similarity is accepted only when a shared usability invariant explains it; visual difference alone is not evidence of generalization.

| Dimension | Goodturn bicycle commerce | Larkhaven municipal service | Relay North warehouse | Morrow literary publication |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Topology | Assortment field → compare/finder → product evidence → reservation/test ride | One request across eligibility, evidence, application, recovery, status, assisted help | Persistent shell around queue, object, history, action, audit | Current issue → lead story → contents → editorial context → archive/membership |
| Navigation | Brand/site destinations, early product access, optional guided finder | Civic frame plus journey-local numbered progress | Stable command rail, filters, search, keyboard traversal | Publication destinations plus issue sequence and in-text continuation |

## Similarities with independent justification

- All four expose visible focus, errors, dialogs, and reduced-motion equivalents because those are interaction-quality invariants, not a Snowe layout.
- All four keep important actions near the evidence that makes them ready. The evidence differs: product fit, eligibility result, affected-object history, or experienced editorial value.
- All four use restrained semantic color. Roles and material behavior differ because contrast, error recognition, and state legibility remain shared accessibility needs.

## Hidden-template audit

No universal topology, hero, card grid, CTA rhythm, imagery posture, custom-asset requirement, motion grammar, or mobile collapse pattern survived comparison. The three new benchmarks do not differ merely by palette: their primary objects, navigation, content sequence, decision moments, and responsive transformations are structurally different.

The main repeated mechanism is the Decision Graph record—driver, move, consequence, evidence, risk, revisit trigger. It constrains justification without prescribing the solution. No affected benchmark required an architecture rewrite after the comparison.
